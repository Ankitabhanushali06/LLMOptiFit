Privacy & service limits

LLMOptiFit is a free pilot for comparing models. No app account is required. Live answer generation, when enabled, uses the app owner’s Nebius account. Visitors do not supply keys or pay the app.

Analyzing a prompt

Choosing Analyze sends your prompt and preferences to the app's Python server. Classification and recommendation happen there without a paid model call. Typing does not send your prompt. The app does not persist prompts or use them for training.

Generating an answer

Choosing Generate sends your prompt and chosen model over HTTPS to this app's server, which forwards them to Nebius Token Factory using the owner's API key. The key stays on the server and is never returned to visitors or written to application logs. You explicitly agree to this sharing before generation.

Nebius processes the request under its own terms and privacy policy. We cannot promise Nebius's retention behavior or erase data from its systems. Review Nebius privacy information and billing terms before submitting sensitive information.

What stays on your device

Prompts and answers stay in the open page; this app has no saved history. Copy and Share send content only where you choose. Closing the page does not guarantee cancellation of an already-sent generation request.

The offline cache contains only public interface files, icons, and this page. API requests, keys, prompts, and answers are excluded. Clearing this site's browser data removes the offline files.

Operational information

Application diagnostics include known endpoint names, status codes, and request timing, never keys or content. Hashed network identifiers are kept in process memory for up to one minute to limit abuse. The hosting provider also processes network information such as IP addresses and request metadata under its own policies. No advertising or analytics scripts are included. See Render's privacy policy.

Free service limits

The free server can sleep after inactivity and take about a minute to restart. Hosting limits may temporarily suspend the service. Prompts are limited to 20,000 characters and generation to 1,024 output tokens. The pilot allows 30 analyses and 5 generation attempts per minute per network address, with two generation requests running at once. These are availability limits, not a guaranteed financial spending cap on the owner’s Nebius account.

Understanding results

Fit scores reflect a heuristic ranking, not measured accuracy. Token and latency predictions are estimates. Costs calculated from usage still use snapshot prices and may differ from the owner’s Nebius invoices. A failed or timed-out generation may have incurred charges; the app never retries it automatically. AI answers may be inaccurate. Review outputs before relying on them.
